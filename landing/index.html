Our story

Built by an athlete. For every athlete.

I'm Mia Bhuta, captain of Stanford Women's Soccer and aspiring to be a professional soccer player.

At 17, I became the first Indian American to represent the U.S. at a FIFA World Cup. I saw an opportunity to build my brand, reaching millions of views and signing an NIL deal with adidas. But managing it all became a full-time job on top of being a student-athlete.

I realized athletes everywhere face the same challenge. They have incredible stories and real opportunities, but not enough time or support to make the most of them.

That's why I built Nomila. To give every athlete personalized support to build their brand, unlock opportunities, and focus on what they do best.

Every athlete has a story. Every story deserves to be shared.

— Mia Bhuta, Founder
