How to delete your Nomila data

Any user, in any region, can delete their data at any time — free of charge. Choose whichever option is easiest for you.

Last updated June 25, 2026  |  Completed within 30 days

Nomila Inc. lets any user, in any region, delete their data at any time, free of charge. You have three options:

Three ways to delete

1Delete in the app Fastest

Open Nomila → Settings → Account → Delete my account. This permanently deletes your profile, connected-account tokens, inbox/deal data, and brand scores, and revokes Nomila's access to your connected services.

2Disconnect a single integration

To remove data from just one connected service (e.g., Gmail or Instagram), open Nomila → Settings → Connections, and tap Disconnect next to that service. We delete the data and tokens associated with that integration.

3Email request

Email privacy@nomila.co from the address on your account with the subject "Delete my data." We will verify your request, delete your data, and send you a confirmation code and the status of your request.

We complete verified deletion requests within 30 days.

01What gets deleted

  • Your account & profile
  • OAuth tokens for connected services
  • Ingested messages and detected deals
  • Generated drafts
  • Your brand scores

Some records may be retained only where required by law (e.g., financial/tax records) or in anonymized, aggregated form that cannot identify you.

02Revoking access at the source

You can also revoke Nomila's access directly with each provider:

  • Google — myaccount.google.com/permissions
  • Meta / Facebook — Settings → Apps and Websites
  • Microsoft — account.microsoft.com privacy
  • TikTok — Settings → Security → Manage app permissions
  • Spotify — Account → Apps

03Questions

Contact privacy@nomila.co and we'll help.
