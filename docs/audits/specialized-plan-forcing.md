## 0. Design under test, in one paragraph

A planner receives the handoff unchanged and the run it names (the Objectives it cites and the Brain's own rendered record, so it reads evidence by reference and copies nothing). It reconciles against Existing Work first, offline: if work already executes the Recommendation it attaches and plans nothing. Otherwise one call returns a **thin envelope** — `recommendationId · planner · result (planned | attached | escalated | refused) · rationale · dependencies by reference · completion {planningCompleteWhen, doesNotImply} · output · escalation` — around a **domain-native output** that the two planners do not share: outreach = target classes, research needed, a gated sequence, materials; content = a system, channels, cadence, a gated sequence, commissions. No `actions[]` exists in either schema (asserted by test). The result projects one Existing Work item linked by `for {recommendationId, objectiveIds}`.

## 1. One harness correction, recorded

The send/publish/spend backstop first matched nouns: "commission … a **pitch** deck" and "draft … **email** templates" were refused as ungated sends in A and B. Both are preparation. The reader was corrected to verbs with an object ("send the first approach to each contact" still refuses; "pitch deck" and "email templates" do not), a regression test carries the forcing run's own phrasings, and the recorded replies were re-gated offline: A 2→0, B 1→0, C 0→0, E 0→0. No rerun. The correction is to the validator's reading, not to what the planners said.

## 2. The five cases

### Case A — Outreach (Yuwa / CSR) · handoff `rec_9feaeb54fb19c6b1`, domain `impact` → outreach planner

**Brain said (unchanged):** "Initiate outreach to global brand CSR departments to secure partnerships co-funding her work with the Yuwa girls empowerment program."
**Planner decided (its layer):** three target classes (global sports and athletic brands, with Adidas as the one candidate the record supports; ed-tech and youth-development corporations; multinational technology and consulting firms with South Asian initiatives); three research needs (CSR budget parameters and criteria → external; Yuwa's current funding needs and scope → athlete; institutional/NIL rules on charitable co-funding → connected); a six-step sequence — research → coordinate with her and Yuwa leadership → **commission** a pitch deck → **[athlete_approval]** present targets and deck → draft templates → **[tap_to_send]** initiate outreach; materials: the deck (`commissions: content`), the outreach sequences (`none`).
**Dependencies cited:** the Objective, `findings[mia.narrative_power]`, `findings[mia.brand_fit]`, `findings[mia.commercial_proof]` — references only.
**Completion:** "external research done, alignment parameters gathered, and she has approved the final pitch deck and target list" · does not imply "any sponsorship or co-funding agreement finalized".
**Rewrite of WHAT/WHY?** No. The move, subject and counterpart are the Brain's; the rationale adds the planner's own ordering (research first because the budget constraint from `notSeen` is the blocker).
**Where HOW appeared:** here, and only here — targets, research, the deck, the templates, the sequence, the gates. The Brain's `what` carried none of it.

### Case B — Positioning / outreach (academic-tech) · handoff `rec_bb66435c91ced059`, domain `outreach`

**Brain said:** "Target premium brand partnerships and ambassador roles within the EdTech, STEM, and technology venture capital sectors."
**Planner decided:** target classes (Stanford-affiliated startups and accelerators, with StartX1 as candidate; premium STEM/ed-tech platforms; technology VC firms); research (partnership structures in tech/VC → external; her boundaries on speculative tech sectors → athlete); a seven-step sequence — research → review the three StartX1 inbound signals → draft a positioning narrative → **[athlete_approval]** → one-pager/deck → templates → **[tap_to_send]** queue messages; materials: one-pager, deck (both `none` — no creative commissioned).
**Dependencies cited:** the Objective, `findings[mia.narrative_power]`, `commercialPosition.inbound[3|6|9]` (the StartX1 signals).
**Completion:** "target classes approved by her, narrative materials finalized, first-contact drafts queued for dispatch" · does not imply "partnerships acquired, deal terms agreed, or sponsored content posted".
**A vs B:** same envelope, same output shape, **materially different plans**: A's spine is a programme partner (Yuwa leadership) and a creative commission; B's spine is warm inbound signals and a positioning narrative with no creative at all. The structure held; the content diverged. That is what "one planner semantic" should look like.
**Two things B exposed (§3 Q2, Q6):** (i) StartX1 is the subject of an Objective **she deferred** ("when I know more") — and the planner made it the first warm lead, because the planner's view contained the state block (where the inbound signals live) but **not her deferred/declined decisions**. The planner did not rewrite the Brain; it planned across a standing athlete decision it could not see. (ii) "Review the StartX1 inbound signals … queue outreach" is a reply to an inbound counterpart already in the pipeline — that is Deals' direct action (`send_reply`), not outreach. The outreach planner reached into the commercial domain at exactly the point where the counterpart is already in motion.

### Case C — Content (TikTok) · handoff `rec_764257cd61ef9777`, domain `content` (the A3 record: `specialized_plan`, pre-R15 wording)

**Brain said (0.7.0-era wording, kept deliberately):** "Create a specialized plan to reactivate her dormant TikTok channel **by cross-posting existing video formats like Reels and carousels**."
**Planner decided:** a system whose role is "reactivate the channel and build consistency by adapting Instagram assets"; channels: Instagram as source library, TikTok as destination; cadence three cross-posts a week; sequence — **[athlete_approval]** audit six months of Instagram content → adapt masters to TikTok specs → **[tap_to_send]** queue and schedule; two commissions (adapt Reels to native clips, 2/week; reformat carousels to vertical clips, 1/week).
**Dependencies:** the Objective, `contentPerformance[tiktok]`, `contentPerformance[instagram]`.
**Completion:** "workflow validated by her, source formats selected, first month of adapted drafts ready for approval" · does not imply "follower rates, virality, or conversion of the dormant account".
**Two findings.** (i) **The residual mechanic in `what` was binding in practice.** The spec told the planner the noun is "one planner's guess"; the planner nonetheless built the entire system around cross-posting and never considered native production except as a later test. A planner does not un-bind a mechanic the Brain stated — which is why R15 had to be closed upstream and why the planner-boundary watch item is a Brain-side discipline, not a planner-side one. (ii) **One crossing into Creative Intelligence, inside a sequence step, not in a commission:** "adapt them to TikTok specs (aspect ratios, **trending audio hooks, caption styling**)". The key-level guard (no `concept`, `hook`, `caption` fields) cannot see prose inside `does`. The commissions themselves stayed at intent level (purpose · format class · cadence · channel). Recorded as the ambiguous case the brief asked for: **adaptation of existing assets** sits on the line — format and cadence are the planner's; hooks and caption style are conception.

### Case D — Existing Work · same handoff as C, with the work overlay executing it

Offline, no call. `alreadyExecuting` found the activation `ctl-r1-tiktok-series` with `for.recommendationId = rec_764257cd61ef9777` and returned `result: "attached"`, `output: null`, `attachedTo: deal[ctl-r1-tiktok-series]`. No parallel plan. The other half, also offline: after Case A's result was projected into Existing Work, re-routing the same governed Recommendation was **refused at dedup** by the unchanged router: "existing work planning_result[outreach:rec_9feaeb54fb19c6b1] is executing this Recommendation — attach, do not create". Two layers, both held, neither collapsed into the other.

### Case E — Campaign escalation · controlled handoff `ctl-rec-matchday-as-plan` (the match-day move shaped as `specialized_plan`)

**Planner returned:** `result: "escalated"`, `output: null`, `escalation: { shape: "campaign", why: "a multi-month recurring activation linked to the fixtures … ending specifically with the season's conclusion", ends: { source: "objective_horizon", ref: "objective[ctl-obj-matchday-series]" } }`. It named the same authoritative source the R17 router resolves, and stopped. Nothing was created: there is no code path from a planner to a campaign, and the router refuses any campaign shape that is not a governed Recommendation with a typed `ends`. One wrinkle, harmless: its `completion` describes "a campaign charter approved by the athlete" — the planner narrating work that is not its own. The envelope's escalation carried what the next Brain/governance cycle needs.

## 3. The fifteen questions

1. **Did the shared PlannerHandoff work unchanged?** Yes. Four handoffs, byte-for-byte as routed, two planners, no field added, none unused. `notSeen` was read as constraints in all four (A built its first step from it; E cited it as a reason to escalate).
2. **Was any Brain semantic missing?** No Brain semantic. One **context** semantic was: the athlete's standing decisions — deferred and declined subjects — were not in the planner's view (B planned across a deferred one). That belongs to the governance record and the handoff *context*, not to the Brain's output; the router can pass `deferredSubjects`/`declinedSubjects` by reference, or the planner prompt can render the run's deferred block. Not a Recommendation field.
3. **Did planner-owned HOW appear in the correct layer?** Yes: targets, research, sequence, gates, materials, cadence, commissions all appeared in the planner and none in the Brain's `what` for A and B. C is the exception that proves it: where the Brain's `what` carried a mechanic, the planner inherited it.
4. **Did any planner rewrite the strategic WHAT/WHY?** No, in all four. Rationales were the planner's own ordering and never a restatement of the move; the validator's strategic-field guard found nothing; the Objectives were cited, not reinterpreted.
5. **Are content and outreach genuinely different planner semantics?** Yes. Different output shapes were needed and used (target classes and research versus system, channels, cadence and commissions), different gates dominate (tap-to-send versus approval and, downstream, spend/publish), and different downstream objects exist (none versus `studio_plans`). A shared schema would have forced one of them to lie.
6. **Does commercial require its own planner semantics?** At the seam, yes, at least as a boundary: B showed an outreach planner drifting into replying to an inbound counterpart already in the pipeline, which is Deals' direct action with its own intent vocabulary and amount discipline. The rule the experiment supports: **a counterpart already in motion is Existing Work for the outreach planner** — hand it to Deals, do not plan an approach. Whether a plan-shaped commercial move (a package across deals) needs a full commercial planner is untested; no case reached it.
7. **Is a shared PlanningResult envelope useful?** Yes, and it stayed thin: result type, rationale, dependencies by reference, completion, escalation, attachedTo. Every one of the six fields did work in at least one case (attachedTo in D, escalation in E, completion in all). Nothing in the envelope describes the work itself.
8. **Did `actions[]` prove generic or domain-specific?** Domain-specific. Outreach's unit is a step toward a counterpart with a send gate; content's unit is a phase plus a commission with a format class. The one thing they share is `{n, does, dependsOn, gate}` on a sequence step — a *dependency and gate* vocabulary, not an action model. Keep that as the shared part; keep the units native.
9. **Is a generic Plan entity still unnecessary?** Yes. What the two planners produced has no common body beyond the envelope; a generic row would hold the envelope and a JSON blob, which is the `tasks`-table failure again.
10. **How is planning completion represented?** As two strings on the envelope, and every planner used them correctly: A/B/C complete at "materials/scope approved by her and drafts ready", never at "partnerships secured" or "followers gained". Planning completion includes her scope approval and stops before any send. The Recommendation's outcome stays the Brain's `MEASURE/LEARN` concern.
11. **How does Existing Work represent planned work?** One `activation` item per planning result, statement in the planner's words ("Outreach planned toward … : 6 steps, none sent yet"), `for {recommendationId, objectiveIds}`, `deliverablesRecorded` = done actions. The next Brain run reads `· executing recommendation[…]`; the router deduplicates on `for`. Demonstrated in D.
12. **Is `ref.kind: "plan"` actually needed?** **Yes for outreach, no for content.** Content's planned work can be projected under its native object once materialised (`studio_plans` → a `studio_plan` kind). Outreach has no native object, so its planning result *is* the work item and needs its own reference kind (`planning_result` in the experiment). So the checkpoint's prediction holds only where no domain object exists — exactly the condition the brief set. Note: `SUBJECT_KINDS` has neither `plan` nor `studio_plan` today; both are additive.
13. **Can the planner escalate to Campaign without converting?** Yes (E). It stopped, named the typed end source, produced no plan, and could not create anything. Conversion is structurally impossible: only a governed campaign-shaped Recommendation with `ends` passes the router.
14. **Where exactly does Creative Intelligence begin?** After the **commission**. The content planner ends at purpose · format class · count-or-cadence · channel · dependency. Concept, hook, angle, script, caption, look, music, references, shots and edits begin on the other side. The one blur (C's "trending audio hooks, caption styling" inside an adaptation step) shows the edge is **asset adaptation**: format and cadence are planning; how the asset reads is conception. A prose-level check on `does` would catch it; a key-level one cannot.
15. **What should the first production planner implementation be?** **The outreach planner, mounted as preparation only**, for three reasons the experiment gives: it is the domain with the most plan-shaped Recommendations (~50 of 100 in the corpus), it has no native object and so cannot be faked through Studio, and its only consequential action (a message) already has a gate model (tap-to-send) — the missing piece is a `pending_actions` kind for an outbound approach. Before it: (a) pass deferred/declined subjects to the planner by reference; (b) the "counterpart already in motion → Deals" rule at the seam; (c) the additive `planning_result` reference kind on the existing-work contract. The content planner waits for Creative Intelligence's edge to be drawn as a contract, since C shows it will otherwise be crossed in prose.

## 4. Backlog

- **P1** — planner context must carry the athlete's deferred/declined subjects by reference (B planned across a deferral). Router/prompt context, not a Brain field.
- **P2** — counterpart already in motion (inbound, negotiating) is Existing Work for the outreach planner: hand to Deals, never plan an approach. A reconcile rule at the seam.
- **P3** — Creative Intelligence edge in prose: a content plan's `does` can carry conception words (hooks, caption style) that the key-level guard cannot see. Draw the edge as a contract before any content planner.
- **P4** — `planning_result` (outreach) and `studio_plan` (content) as additive Existing Work / subject reference kinds when the seam is built.
- **P5** — the residual mechanic in `what` binds planners in practice (C); the planner-boundary watch item is Brain-side, confirmed.
- Send-gate backstop is a lexical reader; it was wrong once and corrected. It is a backstop, never the semantics.

## 5. What was not done, by rule

No production planner. No Campaign, Creative Intelligence or Studio change. No persistence, no Plan or Action table. `athlete_stated` transport untouched. No Brain, Objective, Opportunity, Deals or Rate change — no forcing case exposed a blocking semantic flaw in any of them; the one upstream finding (C) is already closed by R15 for new runs.

## 6. Acceptance (27 September 2026)

Accepted by the user as the Specialized Plan forcing result: Recommendation → PlannerHandoff → specialized planner → domain-native planned work, with no generic Plan entity and no universal Action model. Decision: the first production planner is the **outreach planner, preparation only**; the content planner stays experimental until the Creative Intelligence seam is opened deliberately, with #652 recorded as evidence that content is a distinct semantic, can use `studio_plans`, that Creative Intelligence begins after the commission, and that upstream mechanics in the Brain's `what` bind in practice. Three preconditions closed before mounting: governed exclusions, counterpart ownership, creative ownership — `docs/architecture/commercial-intelligence/outreach-planner-checkpoint.md`.
